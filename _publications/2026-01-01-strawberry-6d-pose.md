The first real-world 6D pose ground truth dataset of strawberries in agricultural fields, paired with a scene-level realistic synthetic dataset built in NVIDIA Isaac Sim. Baseline 6D pose estimation models are evaluated and the sim-to-real gap in agricultural field conditions is analyzed. Conducted as part of the USDA Robotics Strawberry Harvesting Project at the University of Florida, in collaboration with Purdue University.
